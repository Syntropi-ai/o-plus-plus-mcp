# Security

Report suspected vulnerabilities privately to
[eng@syntropi.ai](mailto:eng@syntropi.ai). Include the affected endpoint or
configuration, what you observed and safe reproduction steps.

Do not post vulnerabilities, credentials or private Space content in public
issues. Redact sensitive data from logs and screenshots, and use only accounts
and Spaces you are authorized to test.

For ordinary connection problems, see [Support](SUPPORT.md).
